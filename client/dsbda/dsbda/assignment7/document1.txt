The Indian government launched a tree plantation drive across the country.
Millions of volunteers planted saplings in forests, farms, schools and along riverbanks.
The forest department aims to plant crore saplings all over the state.
Students from schools and colleges participated in the plantation programme.
The government aims to maintain forest cover across the geographical area.
Trees and plants help reduce carbon emissions and combat climate change.
Volunteers registered on the website to participate in the plantation drive.
The forest department created a mobile application to record plantation details.
Officials monitored the progress of the plantation drive across all regions.
India has vowed to have a third of its total land area under forest cover.
